## Coverage Details

### What MetLife Covers

**Accidents & Illness (all plans):**
- Surgeries and procedures
- Emergency care
- Prescription medications
- Diagnostics (X-rays, blood work, etc.)
- Dental coverage
- Chronic/hereditary conditions
- **Rehabilitation therapies (vet-prescribed):**
  - Physical therapy
  - Chiropractic care
  - Hydrotherapy
  - Cold laser
  - Acupuncture

**How Reimbursement Works:**
1. You pay vet bill upfront
2. Submit claim to MetLife
3. Receive 90% reimbursement of eligible expenses
4. After $250 annual deductible is met
5. Capped at $5,000 annual family limit

### What's NOT Covered

- Pre-existing conditions (not covered during waiting period)
- Preventive wellness visits (unless Premium 365/575 add-on)
- Routine exams, vaccines, flea/tick prevention (without add-on)
- Behavioral training (without add-on)

**Note:** Preventive add-ons not recommended - see analysis below.

## Preventive Care Add-Ons: Why We're Skipping Them

MetLife offers optional wellness coverage:

### Preventive 365 Plan
- **Cost:** $50/month extra
- **Max Benefits:** $365/year
- **Verdict:** NOT WORTH IT

### Preventive 575 Plan
- **Cost:** $100/month extra
- **Max Benefits:** $575/year
- **Verdict:** NOT WORTH IT

**Why Not?**

Estimated annual preventive costs for all 3 pets:
- Exams: $250-400
- Vaccines: $110-175
- Heartworm/FeLV prevention: $175-250
- Flea/tick prevention: $190-285
- Health screenings: $200-400
- **Total: $925-1,510/year**

Preventive 365 costs $600/year but only covers $365 = **$235 loss**
Preventive 575 costs $1,200/year but only covers $575 = **$625 loss**

We're better off paying preventive costs out-of-pocket.
