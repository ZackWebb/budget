Metlife offers a "family" plan for multiple animals.

There are sliders for reimbursement amount (70%, 80%, 90%), annual benefit ($2000, $5000, $10000), deductible ($50, $100, $250, $500), and optional preventive care (none, preventive 365, preventive 575)

Metlife suggests 3 combinations:
Balanced - 80%, $2000, $250, no preventative, $58.06 premium
Recommended - 80%, $5000, $250, no preventative, $79.75 premium
High deductible - 80%, $10000, $2500, no preventative, 39.70 premium

I can check other combinations if they are likely to be better for my situation.

It looks like preventative adds around $50 per month for the 365 plan or $100 for the 575 plan
